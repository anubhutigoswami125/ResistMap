READING THE FINE PRINT / 003

Good data
needs context.

ResistMap is an exploration tool, not a clinical decision system. A percentage is meaningful only when you understand what was tested and how the data were collected.

THE WIDER AMR PICTURE ↗
01

ESBL-producing E. coli

Extended-spectrum beta-lactamase (ESBL) is an enzyme that can make bacteria resistant to several important beta-lactam antibiotics. This indicator tracks E. coli bloodstream infection isolates resistant to third-generation cephalosporins.

02

Methicillin-resistant S. aureus

MRSA is a strain of Staphylococcus aureus resistant to methicillin and related antibiotics. This indicator describes MRSA among tested S. aureus bloodstream infection isolates.

03

What the percentage means

These figures refer to patients seeking care whose blood sample was taken and tested. They do not represent every infection, every patient, or the entire population of a country. Testing and coverage may vary.
