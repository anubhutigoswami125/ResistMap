01

What is ESBL?

Extended-spectrum beta-lactamase (ESBL) is an enzyme that can make bacteria resistant to several important beta-lactam antibiotics. This indicator tracks E. coli bloodstream infections resistant to third-generation cephalosporins.

02

What is MRSA?

Methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus (MRSA) is a strain resistant to methicillin and related antibiotics. The indicator describes MRSA among tested S. aureus bloodstream infection isolates.

03

Read responsibly

These are percentages among patients seeking care whose blood sample was taken and tested. They do not represent every infection, every patient, or the entire population of a country.
