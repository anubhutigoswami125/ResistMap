THE WIDER AMR PICTURE ↗
01

ESBL-producing E. coli

Extended-spectrum beta-lactamase (ESBL) is an enzyme that can make bacteria resistant to several important beta-lactam antibiotics. This indicator tracks E. coli bloodstream infection isolates resistant to third-generation cephalosporins.

02

Methicillin-resistant S. aureus

MRSA is a strain of Staphylococcus aureus resistant to methicillin and related antibiotics. This indicator describes MRSA among tested S. aureus bloodstream infection isolates.

03

What the percentage means

These figures refer to patients seeking care whose blood sample was taken and tested. They do not represent every infection, every patient, or the entire population of a country. Testing and coverage may vary.

01 / ABOUT RESISTMAP

A clearer lens on resistance.

ResistMap is an independent educational data-exploration project built to make selected antimicrobial-resistance surveillance indicators easier to explore and interpret. It brings two bloodstream-infection indicators into one place: third-generation cephalosporin resistance in Escherichia coli and methicillin resistance in Staphylococcus aureus.

Explore trends, compare the latest available country observations, and inspect the underlying records. The project is intended for learning and public understanding. It is not affiliated with the World Health Organization, Our World in Data, or any public-health authority.

02 / PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy matters.

ResistMap does not ask you to create an account. The application has no contact form, user profile, advertising tracker, analytics tool, or database for storing personal information. Search terms and chart selections are processed in your browser and are not sent to a ResistMap server.

To display the site, your browser requests files from GitHub Pages. The page also loads typefaces from Google Fonts and photographic assets from Unsplash. Those providers may receive standard technical request information, such as your IP address and browser details, under their own privacy policies. ResistMap does not control their practices.

External links take you to third-party websites. Review their privacy policies before sharing information there. If the site's data-handling practices change, this notice will be updated.
